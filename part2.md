# PART 1
# Reverse Engineering the Problem Statement

## Discovery 1
The title does NOT say

AI Mental Health System

It says

AI-Based Predictive Personnel Stress and Welfare Monitoring System

### Hidden Meaning

The objective is not medical diagnosis.

The objective is organizational welfare.

The platform exists to improve operational readiness.

---

## Discovery 2

Stress is not the root problem.

Operational conditions are.

Operational Conditions

↓

Long Deployments

↓

Behavior Changes

↓

Stress

↓

Reduced Readiness

↓

Mission Risk

Stress is only one stage.

## Discovery 3

The statement asks for

Early Indicators

NOT

Diagnosis

This means

The AI should identify

- Weak signals
- Behavioral drift
- Risk evolution
- Trends

Instead of

Healthy

↓

Stressed

↓

Burnout

## Discovery 4

The document repeatedly mentions

Privacy

Trust

Voluntary Data

Confidentiality

Role-Based Access

### Hidden Meaning

Privacy is NOT an IT feature.

Privacy is a trust-building mechanism.

Without trust

The entire platform fails.

## Discovery 5

- Better welfare
- Better resilience

AI Accuracy
